## Choose your own adventure: Multi-tenant or Single-tenant

IQKV is built to flex with your business, no matter what stage you're in.

### Multi-tenant (default): The smart choice for 99% of businesses

This is the fastest and most cost-effective way to launch. You share the platform infrastructure with other organizations, but every workspace is completely isolated and secure.

Perfect for:

- Startups that want to move fast and validate their idea
- Small to medium businesses that don't need full infrastructure control
- Teams that want to spend their money on product, not servers

Why you'll love it:

- Launch in days, not months
- Lower costs (we split the infrastructure bills!)
- Automatic updates with no work from you
- One place to manage everything

### Single-tenant: Your dedicated platform, your rules

For businesses that need total control, we'll spin up an entire IQKV instance just for you. No shared resources, no compromises.

Perfect for:

- Large enterprises with strict security or compliance rules
- Organizations that need full control over their data and infrastructure
- Businesses that want custom features or integrations

Why you'll love it:

- Complete data isolation — your stuff stays yours
- Customize every part of the platform to fit your needs
- Dedicated support and SLAs
- You decide when updates happen
- Enhanced security and compliance options

## Here's the secret: We use boring, reliable tech so you don't have to care

We built IQKV on microservices and Kubernetes, but you don't need to know what that means. For you, it just translates to benefits:

### Microservices (a team of specialists, not one overworked person)

Instead of one big, fragile app, IQKV is made of small, focused services. For you:

- **Updates without downtime**: We can add new features or fix issues without taking the whole platform down
- **More reliable**: If one small part has a hiccup, everything else keeps working
- **Grows with you**: As you get more users, we can scale just the parts that need it

### Kubernetes-first (your platform's personal IT team)

Kubernetes is like a super-smart manager for our services. For you:

- **Always on**: If something breaks, it automatically fixes itself
- **Scales without effort**: From 10 users to 10,000, it just works
- **Secure by default**: We follow the best security practices so you don't have to
- **Consistent everywhere**: It works the same in development, testing, and production
- **Fast updates**: New features roll out smoothly without disrupting your users

## Quick tips to get the most out of IQKV

Here are some of our favorite hacks to make your IQKV experience even better:

### For workspace owners

- Keep your team organized with clear roles — only give Owner access to people you really trust
- Regularly check pending invitations and revoke ones you don't need
- Use the Stripe billing portal to download invoices for your accounting
- Give your organization a clear, memorable name so members can find it easily

### For platform operators

- Use audit logs to troubleshoot issues or verify important actions
- Create plans with clear, easy-to-understand tiers to help your users choose
- Use announcements to keep users in the loop about updates or maintenance
- Keep an eye on subscription metrics to track your platform's growth

### For everyone

- Always verify your email — it unlocks all the features!
- Use a strong password that's unique to IQKV
- Sign out of shared devices when you're done
- Check your notifications regularly — we only send important stuff
