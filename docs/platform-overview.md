# Platform Overview

IQKV is a modern multi-tenant SaaS platform designed to give you your own isolated workspace in seconds. Here's what you need to know.

---

## Two main apps

The platform has two separate interfaces, each for a different audience:

| App                | URL             | Who uses it?                |
| ------------------ | --------------- | --------------------------- |
| **Tenant App**     | app.iqkv.site   | Regular users, team members |
| **Platform Admin** | admin.iqkv.site | Platform operators          |

---

## Tenant App (app.iqkv.site)

This is where you spend most of your time as a user. Key features:

- **Sign up & sign in** — create your account in seconds, get your personal workspace
- **Workspaces** — switch between personal workspace and organizations you belong to
- **Teams** — invite members, assign roles, manage access
- **Billing** — subscribe to plans, manage payments, view invoices
- **Account settings** — update profile, change password, set preferences
- **Notifications** — stay in the loop with in-app alerts

---

## Platform Admin (admin.iqkv.site)

For the people who run the platform. They can:

- **Dashboard** — see big-picture stats (total users, orgs, subscriptions)
- **User management** — view, edit, ban/unban any user across the platform
- **Organization management** — oversee workspaces, manage members, billing
- **Invitations** — send, track, revoke invitations globally
- **Subscriptions** — view all active subscriptions and their details
- **Plans** — create and manage the pricing plans offered to tenants
- **Announcements** — send platform-wide announcements
- **Audit logs** — review what happened across the system
- **Refunds** — view and manage refund history
- **My account** — manage operator profile and password

---

## Workspace types

You get two kinds of workspaces:

- **Personal workspace** — your private space, always free
- **Organization workspace** — for teams, requires a subscription (except free plans)

---

## Roles

Inside an organization workspace, there are three roles:

| Role       | What they can do                                                                    |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Owner**  | Full control — invite/remove members, set roles, manage billing, transfer ownership |
| **Admin**  | Can manage members and settings (coming soon)                                       |
| **Member** | Basic access — use features, view team, manage own account                          |

---

## Sign-up flow

1. Go to app.iqkv.site
2. Click Sign up
3. Enter name, email, password
4. Verify your email
5. Start using your personal workspace

That's it! You can create an organization workspace later if you want to collaborate with others.
