# The Platform Admin Console: Your Control Center

Welcome to [admin.iqkv.site](https://admin.iqkv.site) — your one-stop shop for managing your entire IQKV platform. This is where operators like you keep things running smoothly, help your users, and watch your business grow.

---

## Let's get you logged in

### Signing in

Head to [admin.iqkv.site](https://admin.iqkv.site) and use your operator credentials to sign in. Regular tenant users won't have access here — only accounts with the special "PLATFORM_ADMIN" role can log in.

### Quick note on session security

Just like the tenant app, your session will expire after 30 minutes of inactivity. We use the same secure token handling with silent refreshes in the background — you usually won't even notice it's happening!

---

## Dashboard: Your morning coffee companion

After you sign in, you'll land right here with three key stats at your fingertips:

- Total users on your platform
- Total organizations
- Active subscriptions

Each card loads independently, so if one is having a hiccup, the rest keep working perfectly.

---

## Users: Keep your user community happy

This is where you manage every user across your entire platform.

### User list: Find anyone fast

- Paginated, sortable, and filterable — no endless scrolling!
- Search by name or email to locate someone quickly
- Filter by user status to see who you're looking for

### User detail view: The full picture

Click on any user to dive in with two handy tabs:

- **Overview**: See their profile, account status, and take quick actions
- **Organizations**: See all the workspaces they're part of and their roles there

### User actions: Help your users when need it

From the detail view, you've got the power to:

- Edit a user's profile information
- Set a new password for them (careful with this one!)
- Ban or unban a user
- Unlock an account that got locked

---

## Organizations: Keep your workspaces healthy

Manage every single organization across your platform from here.

### Organization list: See everything at a glance

- Paginated list with status filters (active, suspended, etc.)
- Search by organization name or unique key to find what you need

### Organization detail view: Dive deep

Each organization has five helpful tabs:

- **Overview**: Edit the organization's display name and metadata
- **Members**: View and manage workspace members and their roles
- **Billing**: See and edit tenant billing settings
- **Subscriptions**: Look at their subscription history and current plan
- **Refunds**: See any refunds for this organization

## Quick tips to be a platform admin pro

### Running your platform smoothly

- Start each day with a quick glance at the dashboard to see how things are growing
- Check audit logs regularly to spot trends or troubleshoot issues
- Keep your operator profile up to date
- Change your operator password every few months for security

### Helping your users

- Use the search and filter tools to find users fast
- Try to resolve issues with users before banning them as a last resort
- If someone gets locked out, unlock them right away
- Be super careful when setting passwords for users — only do it when absolutely necessary

### Managing organizations

- Keep an eye on organization activity to make sure everyone's following your policies
- Review subscription statuses regularly to catch potential issues early
- Keep organization metadata accurate and up to date
- Use the member management tools to help organizations stay organized

### Billing and plans

- Create plans that are clear and easy understand — explain exactly what's included at each tier
- Test any plan changes in a safe environment before rolling them out to everyone
- Keep an eye on refund requests to spot any patterns or issues with your service
- Use the subscription list to track your revenue and growth over time

### Communicating with your users

- Use announcements to share important updates, maintenance windows, or exciting new features
- Keep announcements short, clear, and friendly
- If you have users in multiple countries, consider translating announcements!

### Keeping your platform secure

- Guard your operator credentials like gold — they give full access to everything!
- Don't share operator accounts — every person who needs admin access should have their own
- Use a strong, unique password for your operator account
- Always sign out of the admin console when you're not using it, especially on shared or public computers
